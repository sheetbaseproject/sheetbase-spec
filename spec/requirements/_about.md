Each requirement concept carries one area's requirements as a table; `requirement_id` in the
frontmatter is the area prefix (the table rows carry the numbered ids). Priority means: `must`
blocks v1, `should` is planned for v1 and may slip to v1.1 with a recorded reason, `could` is
opportunistic.
