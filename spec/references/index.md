# Overview

* [About the references](_about.md) - The large tables that do not fit a design concept: the xldatagrid traceability table, the gridcraft harvest list, the Grist source map and the licence inventory.

# Reference

* [gridcraft harvest list](gridcraft-harvest.md) - File-level list of what sheetbase copies, adapts or rejects from storytold/gridcraft at the audited commit, with the destination and reason, under the Apache-2.0 branch of its MIT OR Apache-2.0 licence.
* [Grist source map](grist-source-map.md) - Which grist-core file each sheetbase module reimplements (behaviour, not code), so a reader of the Grist source can find our counterpart and vice versa.
* [Licence inventory](licences.md) - Every upstream and dependency licence that matters, the obligation it creates, and where in the repos the obligation is met.
* [xldatagrid traceability table](xldatagrid-traceability.md) - Every xldatagrid component, behaviour module and extension mapped to a sheetbase crate and module with a status of port, merge-with-grist, or drop and the reason.
